Step 1: Document Ingestion (Reading Files)

Loads raw files from disk, cloud, or URLs and converts them into clean, readable text with basic metadata (e.g., filename, page number, size).

LLMs don't know your private documents. Reading the files is the first step to teaching the AI your company's data.
